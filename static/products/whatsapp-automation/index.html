Questions about WhatsApp Automation

What is WhatsApp Business automation?

It brings repeatable messaging tasks such as template management, campaign sending, and delivery reporting into one workflow.

Do I need an approved WhatsApp template?

Use a template approved by WhatsApp where its Business Messaging Policy requires it. The applicable requirements depend on how the conversation started and when you send the message.

Can I track campaign delivery?

Yes. The WhatsApp dashboard shows campaign activity such as messages sent, pending, failed, or delivered.

Can my team handle customer replies?

Yes. LeadWoo brings replies into the shared workspace so your team can review and continue conversations.
