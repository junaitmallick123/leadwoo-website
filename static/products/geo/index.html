Questions about GEO · AI Search Visibility

What is Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of measuring and improving how a brand and its content are represented in AI-generated answers. It can include tracking brand mentions, competitors, and cited sources.

What does the AI Visibility Check cover?

It reviews answers to selected buyer questions for your brand, competitors, and cited sources. The findings help prioritise content improvements.

Which AI platforms does LeadWoo review?

The AI Visibility Check can review ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude. The review covers the selected prompts and reflects the responses observed during the check.

How is GEO related to SEO?

SEO helps make pages discoverable and useful in search. GEO adds a view of whether those pages or brands are used, cited, or mentioned in generated answers.
