Questions about LinkedIn Automation

What is LinkedIn outreach automation?

It is a way to organise the repeatable parts of LinkedIn prospecting, such as audience lists, connection sequences, and follow-ups, so your team can focus on the conversations.

Can I use an existing prospect list?

Yes. You can build or upload a targeted list for a campaign and plan the outreach steps for that audience.

How do branching sequences work?

An acceptance branch lets the campaign use a relevant follow-up path after a connection is accepted. You can review campaign status and replies from LeadWoo.

What should I check before using LinkedIn outreach tools?

Review LinkedIn’s rules for automated activity and use account workflows that you are authorised to run. LeadWoo can walk you through its campaign setup in a demo.
