Questions about Email Automation

What is email outreach automation?

It helps teams organise lists, schedule multi-step email outreach, and track activity and replies from one workspace.

Can I manage multiple email inboxes?

Yes. LeadWoo supports managing multiple sending inboxes and domains as part of your email outreach setup.

Does inbox warm-up guarantee deliverability?

Inbox placement depends on factors such as sender reputation, authentication, list quality, and recipient engagement. Warm-up is one part of preparing a sending setup.

What can I measure in an email campaign?

Review sends and replies at the campaign level. Use address verification and bounce activity to see where your list or sending setup needs attention.
