PRIVACY AT LEADWOO

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how LeadWoo collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website, connected outreach products, AI agents and related services.

1. Scope and our data roles

This Privacy Policy applies to LeadWoo (“LeadWoo”, “we”, “our” or “us”), including our website, dashboards, LinkedIn, Email and WhatsApp automation, Voice AI Agent, Marketing AI Agent (Woo Agent), CRM features, APIs and support services (together, the “Services”).

Important distinction. For account, billing, website and support information, LeadWoo generally acts as the organisation deciding why and how the data is used. For prospect, campaign, message, contact, call or CRM data uploaded or connected by a customer, the customer generally determines the purpose of processing and LeadWoo processes that data on the customer’s instructions.

Customers are responsible for ensuring they have a lawful basis, required notices and permissions for prospecting, outreach, call recording, messaging and marketing activities conducted through the Services.

Sources of information

We receive information directly from you, from your authorised workspace users, from connected services you choose to integrate, from your use of the Services, and from service providers supporting payments, authentication, analytics, hosting and security.

3. How and why we use information

We use information to provide and secure the Services; authenticate users; send customer-authorised communications; manage subscriptions; provide support; detect replies, bounces, fraud and misuse; analyse reliability and performance; comply with law; enforce agreements; and develop features.

Where applicable, our legal bases may include performance of a contract, your consent, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving a business service. Where consent is the applicable basis, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

We do not sell personal data. We also do not use Gmail content for advertising or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising unless separately disclosed and permitted by applicable law.

4. AI, automation and model use

LeadWoo uses automated systems to execute customer-configured workflows, draft or recommend content, analyse campaign performance, classify replies, support conversations and generate other requested outputs. AI outputs may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed before use where decisions could materially affect a person.

  • We do not use sensitive, proprietary customer content to train public or general-purpose AI models.
  • Authorised subprocessors may process limited data to deliver AI functionality under contractual and security controls.
  • LeadWoo does not use AI to make legally significant decisions about users on its own behalf. Customers must assess and lawfully configure any use that could significantly affect individuals.
  • Human access is limited to authorised personnel who need it for support, security, debugging, legal compliance or with your permission.

5. Google and Gmail data

LeadWoo connects to Google accounts through Google OAuth only after a user grants permission. We request access needed to operate email outreach and campaign features.

Gmail send access

Send permission allows emails to be sent from a connected Gmail account only in response to campaigns, actions or automations configured or approved by the user.

Gmail read-only access

Read-only access may be used to inspect relevant inbox, spam and thread information for reply detection, bounce detection, campaign tracking and stopping follow-up sequences after a reply.

Data that may be accessed

Sender and recipient addresses, subject lines, timestamps, thread IDs, labels or folders, delivery information and message content when needed for reply detection, bounce handling or campaign tracking.

Use, sharing and human access

  • Gmail data is used to send outreach, detect replies and bounces, stop automated follow-ups and update campaign status.
  • Gmail data is not sold or used for advertising.
  • It is shared only with service providers necessary to deliver the feature, to meet legal or security obligations, or with your consent.
  • Team members do not read message content except for authorised support, debugging, security, legal compliance or with explicit permission.

Disconnecting and deletion

You may disconnect Gmail in LeadWoo or revoke access through your Google Account’s third-party app settings. Once disconnected, new Gmail access stops. You may request deletion of stored Gmail-related data by emailing vishal@getleadwoo.com, subject to limited legal, security and backup retention.

LeadWoo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

6. Cookies and tracking choices

We may use strictly necessary cookies for authentication and security, preference cookies for settings, and analytics technologies to understand website and product performance. You can manage cookies through browser settings and, where provided, our consent controls. Disabling necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Services from working.

We do not respond to every legacy “Do Not Track” signal. Where legally required, we honour recognised opt-out preference signals for covered sale or sharing activities.

7. How we share information

We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers and subprocessors supporting hosting, storage, authentication, communications, payments, analytics, customer support, AI processing and security.
  • Workspace administrators who manage your organisation’s LeadWoo account.
  • Connected platforms when you direct us to send, retrieve or synchronise data.
  • Legal and safety recipients where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, users, the public or the Services.
  • Business transaction recipients in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, restructuring or sale, subject to appropriate confidentiality.

International transfers

Information may be processed in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use recognised contractual safeguards, transfer mechanisms and supplementary protections, and we follow restrictions notified under applicable Indian law.

8. Retention, security and incidents

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including the duration of an account or contract, customer instructions, backup cycles, security needs, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Retention differs by data type and feature. After deletion, limited copies may remain in protected backups until overwritten.

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including encryption in transit and where appropriate at rest, access controls, authentication, logging, monitoring, secure infrastructure and vendor reviews. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

If a personal-data breach occurs, we will investigate, mitigate and notify affected users, customers or authorities when required by applicable law. Customers remain responsible for any notices connected to data for which they determine the purpose and means of processing.

9. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live and which law applies, you may have rights to access or know about personal data; correct inaccuracies; request deletion or erasure; obtain a portable copy; withdraw consent; object to or restrict processing; opt out of sale, sharing or certain targeted advertising; limit specified uses of sensitive data; nominate another person where available; and complain to a regulator or supervisory authority.

India

Where the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and related rules apply, Data Principals may exercise applicable rights to access information, correction, completion, updating, erasure, grievance redressal and nomination, subject to the law’s commencement timetable and permitted exceptions.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom

Where GDPR-style laws apply, you may also object to certain legitimate-interest processing, request restriction, receive data portability and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. states

Where applicable, residents may request to know, access, correct or delete personal information and may opt out of covered sale, sharing, targeted advertising or profiling. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

How to submit a request

Email vishal@getleadwoo.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. Describe the request and the account or workspace involved. We may verify your identity and authority, and an authorised agent may be required to provide proof. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

10. Children’s privacy

The Services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal data for their own use of the Services. If you believe a child’s data was submitted improperly, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Third-party services and links

Our Services may link to or integrate with third-party products. Their own terms and privacy notices govern their independent processing, and LeadWoo is not responsible for third-party privacy practices outside our control.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our Services, vendors or legal obligations change. We will post the revised date and provide additional notice through the Services or by email when a change is material and the law requires notice.

This policy describes our intended practices and is not a substitute for customer-specific contractual terms, a Data Processing Addendum, or legal advice. If a contract conflicts with this policy, the contract governs to the extent stated in it.
